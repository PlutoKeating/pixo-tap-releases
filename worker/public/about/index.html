About us

Page updated: 2026-10-12

Who we are

PIXO TAP is desktop software built by a small team. It helps you pick up progress across several long-running tasks. Once you start recording, the software organises your work context on your device and writes recovery notes, so you can carry on quickly next time.

Team and operator details: [team description and operator name, to be provided by the owner].

Our principles

  • Minimal collection. Nothing is recorded by default. Recording starts only when you click start and grant permission in the system, and each kind of collection can be turned off at any time.
  • Local first. Original screenshots, photos, original file contents and clipboard text stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • No sale of data, no advertising tracking. The website has no ads and no third-party tracking scripts, and the public pages set no cookies.
  • Consent can be withdrawn and data can be taken with you. In the account centre you can withdraw consent, export your data and request account deletion (deletion has a 14-day grace period that you can cancel).
  • Limits stated plainly. Desktop recognition on macOS and Linux covers the whole screen, so tidy your desktop first. On GNOME Wayland the foreground window cannot be read.
